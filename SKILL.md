# Requirements Grilling

Use this skill before substantial work when the desired outcome or decision is not fully specified.

## Workflow

1. State the decision or outcome being clarified.
2. Ask one small batch of questions at a time, starting with the highest-impact unknowns.
3. Separate facts, preferences, constraints, and unresolved decisions.
4. Challenge ambiguous terms with concrete examples and counterexamples.
5. Record agreed decisions, non-goals, risks, and acceptance criteria.
6. Stop when another implementer can proceed without inventing product behavior.

If a repository is present, offer to record durable terminology and decisions through `domain-context`. Do not silently create external tickets or send messages.

## Completion

The final output must include the agreed goal, audience, in-scope behavior, out-of-scope behavior, constraints, success criteria, and remaining assumptions.
